OpenComputers printer textures
Source: https://github.com/MightyPirates/OpenComputers/tree/master-MC1.12
Original files: assets/opencomputers/textures/blocks/printer_side.png and printer_top.png
Adaptation: cropped material regions, repacked UV atlas and neutral runtime emission masks.
Used by the photopolymer printer and loot collector. No OpenComputers code is included.
