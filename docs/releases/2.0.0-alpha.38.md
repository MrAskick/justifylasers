# 2.0.0-alpha.38 — Production and tablet guide

- Unified configured schematics into one data-driven item. Added clearing in a crafting grid; blank cards remain separate.
- Rebalanced amplifiers with individual 512px icons. Tiers I–IV are available; V–VI are temporarily hidden without deleting existing items. Emitters accept 1–64 equal-tier amplifiers, with output and FE cost scaling per item. Each tier multiplies both by eight, starting at 32 klm / 34 FE/t.
- Replaced origin-tag growth restrictions with consumable seeds crafted from a gem and raw photonite. Recycling a gem cannot replenish the raw substrate. Cut gems are ordinary resources again.
- Corrected photonite seeds and their required growth spectrum to purple.
- Restored exact-filter extraction from the Laser Cutter, including legacy output stacks, through every face of its eight member blocks.
- Reworked Spectrum Module normals, inventory framing and neutral materials. Replaced the collection module with a storage chest and a live contents display.
- Reworked Laser Cutter textures and corrected overlapping rail/carriage surfaces without replacing its geometry.
- Added the current recipe's minimum light requirement to the grower GUI. Internal rays now appear only during production.
- Added a client toggle for the refocusing cube's inner sphere.
- Added Help and Schematics tabs to the handheld tablet, with a searchable, scrollable English/Russian guide covering progression and every active item.
- Fixed universal-JAR forwarding of JEI subtype registration, so schematics and amplifier tiers stay distinct in the catalog. Updated JEI recipes, legacy-item migration and regression coverage.

Back up worlds before updating. Old schematic IDs remain loadable; retired amplifier tiers 7–15 become tier VI. Existing seed wear and paid growth progress are preserved. Clients and servers must use the same mod version.
