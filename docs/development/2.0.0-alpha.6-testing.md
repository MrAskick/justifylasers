# 2.0.0-alpha.6 verification

Checked on 2026-09-10 with Corretto 22.0.2 and Gradle 8.14.3 on Windows.

## Build and tests

`gradlew.bat build --offline --console=plain` builds Fabric/Forge 1.20.1 and Fabric/NeoForge 1.21.1. All 37 JUnit tests and 112 Fabric 1.20.1 GameTests pass. The three new unit tests cover button-state priority, distinct bounded icon meshes with GUI-facing winding, and convex frame geometry within existing button bounds.

The powered-GUI client harness passes on Fabric 1.20.1 and 1.21.1:

- All six pages in English/Russian, at GUI scales 2 and 3, including an 854×480 window.
- Mouse activation, keyboard focus and Enter activation, Escape/back navigation.
- Disabled module settings and read-only security controls reject both mouse and keyboard activation.
- Player inventory remains visible on every page; slot frames and controls do not overlap or cross the GUI/window bounds.

Screenshots cover the normal, hovered, keyboard-focused, selected, and disabled states. Final icon spacing and label padding were rechecked on Fabric 1.20.1. Useful captures in `platforms/fabric-1.20.1/build/run/clientSmoke/screenshots/` are `en-powered-main-large.png`, `en-energy-large.png`, `ru-damage-compact.png`, and `ru-readonly-security.png`.

The Forge 1.20.1 world harness passes with Oculus 1.8.0, Embeddium 0.3.31, and Kappa_v5.3_Caves. The real powered-emitter menu opens, synchronizes its owner/settings/inventory and energy cost, and retains its transparent background. `SCORCH_WORLD_SMOKE_PASSED shader=true`, `NETWORK_MENU_SMOKE_PASSED`, and `CRYSTAL_WORLD_SMOKE_PASSED shader=true` are recorded in the isolated client-smoke log. Shader captures are `scorch-kappa-network-menu.png` and `scorch-kappa-network-energy-menu.png` in that platform's screenshot folder. Native integration and extended multiplayer tests were not rerun for this visual-only patch.

## Scope

The changed release classes are limited to `PoweredLaserEmitterScreen` and its new `TechGui` drawing helper. JAR comparison against alpha.5 confirms that gameplay classes, network code, recipes, translations, textures, and materials are byte-for-byte unchanged. The old nested bitmap-glyph enum is replaced by the shared scalable icon meshes. No smoke/test classes are bundled.

Button hitboxes, screen dimensions, inventory positions, slider ranges and packet submission logic are unchanged. The creative-emitter and receiver screens keep their existing design. The supplied transparent background PNG is unchanged; no new raster assets or dependencies are introduced.

The previous source tree and all four alpha.5 JARs are preserved in `backups/before-gui-buttons-2.0.0-alpha.5-20260910-142540/`. Client checks use isolated development profiles and do not modify the user's launcher mods or ordinary worlds.
