# 2.0.0 implementation

Target matrix: Minecraft 1.20.1 (Fabric, Forge), Minecraft 1.21.1 (Fabric, NeoForge).

## Acceptance checks

- Preserve the standalone emitter, receivers, cubes, mining settings, and shader rendering.
- Share gameplay between loaders; isolate registration, networking, energy, and rendering hooks.
- Add a separate energy-powered emitter when a supported technical mod is present.
- In technical mode, remove the standalone emitter's survival recipe without changing its registry ID.
- Require a crafted color crystal before the powered emitter can operate.
- Gate Silk Touch, block drops, scorch marks, and ignition behind crafted inventory modules.
- Charge energy on the server; configurable costs increase with mining speed, damage rate, and knockback.
- Preserve inventory and energy in saves, synchronize the GUI, and stop all beam effects without power.
- Use the platform energy API; make technical-mode detection configurable, with Mekanism among the defaults.
- Verify each target, including dedicated server, client, packet validation, and applicable shader/energy integrations.
