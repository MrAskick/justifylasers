# 2.0.0-alpha.21

Changes since the last published version, **2.0.0-alpha.6**.

## Added

- Universal JARs for Minecraft 1.20.1 and 1.21.1, each supporting Fabric, Forge and NeoForge.
- Mirrors, six-port splitters, energy receivers, crystal color mixing, a Configurator and target filters.
- Laser Gun, automated turret, LightSaber and Light Staff; aiming, independent dual wielding, directional attacks, guards, parries, stamina and blade clashes.
- Wolframite and Photonite ores, animated growth/assembly multiblocks, five laser components and 26 schematic-based assembly recipes.
- Rechargeable handheld tablet for recording schematics, with a live clock, battery display and rotatable item previews.
- Client graphics/audio settings, optional Jade/JEI integration, weapon scorch trails and 22 new saber recordings.

## Changed / removed

- Reworked models, UVs, emissive materials and lenses; fixed clipping, shader/sky visibility, scope rendering and multiblock culling.
- Replaced advanced-device workbench recipes with assembly; replaced old chassis/optics parts with compatible housing/lens equivalents. Assembly time and energy now vary by recipe.
- Removed unused textures and four old saber recordings; excluded private reference assets from publication.

Still alpha: the tablet's discovery structure is not implemented; generator/smelter recipes remain disabled (WIP). Guns, turrets and sabers do not consume energy yet.
